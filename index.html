All three wavelengths are delivered together from one applicator with SHR “in-motion” technology (low energy, high pulse rate, constantly gliding) and ICE cooling for comfort. Results vary. Very light, grey or red hair may respond less, so a consultation is recommended.

FAQ

Good to know

What services do you offer?

Laser hair removal, permanent makeup, facials, skin care treatments, threading, waxing, eyelash extensions, lash lift and tint, and makeup application.

How can I book an appointment?

Book online right here on our website, or contact us by phone, text, email, WhatsApp or direct message on social media.

Do I have to pay when I book?

For services $100 and up, you can pay a 20% deposit or the full amount online. For services under $100, you can pay online or in person at the studio.

What should I expect during my visit?

Personalised service, a relaxing atmosphere and expert care to enhance your natural beauty.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

It is described as long-term hair reduction rather than guaranteed permanent removal. Multiple sessions are needed, and maintenance treatments may be recommended.

How should I prepare for laser?

Shave the area, avoid waxing, threading or plucking, and avoid tanning and excess sun. Tell us about any medications or recent treatments.

Is laser hair removal painful?

Sensation varies by area and person. The Soprano ICE Platinum uses SHR technology and ICE cooling for a comfort-focused treatment.

How many laser sessions will I need?

It depends on hair colour, thickness, area and skin tone. Many clients need a series; for men’s back and chest, typically 6 to 8 sessions about 6 to 8 weeks apart.

Does laser work on all skin tones and hair colours?

The multi-wavelength system, including Nd:YAG, suits a wide range of skin tones. Blonde, grey and red hair have less pigment and may respond less, so we assess you at your consultation.

Do you offer men’s laser hair removal in Calgary?

Yes. We treat the back, chest, shoulders, arms, legs, beard line and neck, with settings suited to denser hair.

Where are you located?

Evanston, NW Calgary, Alberta (T3P 1E3). Call or text 403-415-6294.
